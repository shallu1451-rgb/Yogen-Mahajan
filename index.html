Return & Refund Policy

Important information about returns and refunds at our store

Home›Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Thank you for shopping at Ritu Mahajan Fashion Store, Booth No. 306, Sadar Bazar, Sector 19-C, Chandigarh. We want you to be completely satisfied with every purchase. Please read this Return & Refund Policy carefully before making a purchase at our store.

📌 Important Note

This is a physical store only. We do not sell products online. All purchases are made in-person at our store. There is no online payment, no e-commerce checkout, and no delivery service. This policy applies exclusively to in-store purchases.

1. Exchange Policy

We offer exchanges on eligible items within 7 days of purchase, subject to the following conditions:

  • The item must be unused, unwashed and in its original condition with all tags intact.
  • The original purchase receipt / bill must be presented at the time of exchange.
  • The item must be brought to our store at Booth No. 306, Sadar Bazar, Sector 19-C, Chandigarh.
  • Exchanges are subject to availability of the desired item in our current stock.
  • Exchange requests made after 7 days from the date of purchase will not be accepted.

2. Refund Policy

Refunds are offered in the following circumstances only:

  • Manufacturing Defect: If a product has a genuine manufacturing defect that was not visible at the time of purchase, a full refund or exchange will be provided upon verification.
  • Wrong Item: If we have given you an incorrect item that does not match what was billed, a full refund or replacement will be arranged.
  • Refunds will be processed in the same mode of payment used at the time of purchase (cash for cash purchases).
  • Refund requests must be made within 3 days of purchase with the original bill.

3. Non-Returnable & Non-Refundable Items

The following items cannot be returned, exchanged or refunded:

  • Items that have been stitched, altered, washed or used.
  • Unstitched fabrics and dress materials that have been cut to a specific measurement.
  • Sale items, discounted items or items purchased during special promotional offers.
  • Items without their original tags, packaging or purchase receipt.
  • Items purchased more than 7 days ago (for exchange) or 3 days ago (for refund).
  • Items returned due to a change of mind, colour preference or personal taste — we encourage customers to check thoroughly before purchasing.

4. Conditions for Return / Exchange

To be eligible for a return or exchange, please ensure:

  • ✅ Item is in its original, unused condition.
  • ✅ All original tags and labels are attached.
  • ✅ Original purchase bill / receipt is presented.
  • ✅ Request is made within the eligible time period.
  • ✅ Item is brought physically to our store.

5. How to Request a Return or Exchange

To initiate a return or exchange, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 98889 60332 with your purchase details and reason for return.
  2. Our team will guide you and confirm if your item is eligible.
  3. Visit our store at Booth No. 306, Sadar Bazar, Sector 19-C, Chandigarh with the item and original bill.
  4. Our staff will inspect the item and process the exchange or refund accordingly.

6. Colour & Fabric Variation

Please note that slight colour variations may occur due to lighting conditions, photography, screen resolution, and natural fabric dye differences. These are not considered defects and are not eligible for return or refund. We strongly recommend viewing the product carefully in-store before purchase.

7. Governing Law

This Return & Refund Policy is governed by the laws of India and applicable Consumer Protection Act regulations. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Chandigarh, India.

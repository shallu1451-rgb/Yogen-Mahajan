Indian Artistry

Where Print Meets Intricate Embroidery

The true charm of Indian ethnic fashion lies in the details of hand-guided needlework. At Ritu Mahajan Fashion Store in Sadar Bazar, Sector 19-C, Chandigarh, our Printed & Embroidered suits celebrate Punjab’s rich Phulkari heritage alongside intricate Kashmiri Aari needlework and Rajasthani Gota Patti.

Every piece in this collection features artistic digital or block prints elevated with textural embroidery on the neckline, sleeve hems, and daman borders. Accompanied by embroidered chinon or organza dupattas, these suits are cherished by women of all generations across Chandigarh.

Needlework Standards

The Needlework Guarantee at Ritu Mahajan

Why our embroidery stays intact and holds its beauty for generations.

🪡

Tightly Knotted Embroidery

No loose hanging threads or unraveling stitches. All backings are properly locked and fused with fine interlining to protect the embroidery.

🧵

Fast Dyed Silk Threads

We use colorfast silk and polyester threads that do not bleed onto white or pastel fabrics during routine laundering or dry cleaning.

🏷️

Direct Weaver & Artisan Sourcing

We source directly from artisan clusters in Amritsar, Patiala, Lucknow, and Jaipur, saving you intermediate distributor markups.

👗

Full 3-Piece Meterage

Both our unstitched and ready-to-wear sets are designed with ample fabric allowance so you can stitch heavy Punjabi salwars or straight pants.
